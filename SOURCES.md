# Medical references

This prototype uses concise paraphrases of public first-aid guidance. It does not reproduce source diagrams or long passages. Source organizations retain ownership of their publications and materials.

## 1. Resuscitation Council UK — First Aid Guidelines (2025)
https://www.resus.org.uk/professional-library/2025-resuscitation-guidelines/first-aid-guidelines

Primary reference for the initial adult choking, severe bleeding, and suspected stroke guidance.

## 2. Resuscitation Council UK — Adult Basic Life Support Guidelines (2025)
https://www.resus.org.uk/professional-library/2025-resuscitation-guidelines/adult-basic-life-support-guidelines

Reference for the adult unresponsive / not breathing normally and CPR topic.

## 3. UK Government — 999 and 112: The UK's National Emergency Numbers
https://www.gov.uk/guidance/999-and-112-the-uks-national-emergency-numbers

Reference for the UK emergency contact numbers used in this demonstration.

## Clinical review required
This is a simplified educational summary, not a substitute for complete guidelines. Before public use, a qualified clinical or first-aid reviewer must verify every instruction, decision branch, age boundary, and emergency action against current guidance. Record reviewer identity, qualifications, and review date only after an actual review.
